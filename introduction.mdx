## Core Concepts

### Agents
Voice agents are the core of the platform. Each agent has its own personality, voice, language, and capabilities defined through system prompts and tools.

### Tools
Tools extend your agent's capabilities. They can make HTTP requests to your APIs, trigger webhooks, or execute client-side actions during a call.

### Knowledge Bases
Train your agents with domain-specific knowledge by uploading documents, URLs, or text content.

### Phone Numbers
Assign phone numbers to agents to receive inbound calls, or use the API to initiate outbound calls.
